What file format do I get?

Every template is a standard Excel workbook (.xlsx). No macros, no add-ins, no installer. It works in Excel 2010 and later on Windows and Mac, and in LibreOffice Calc.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes, by import. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets, or use File, Import inside Sheets. Formulas, color coding, dropdowns and conditional formatting carry over. Print layouts may need a quick adjustment because Sheets handles page setup differently than Excel.

How is it licensed? Can my whole crew use it?

Each purchase is licensed for use by the purchaser's business. Use it on as many of your own machines as you need and share it with the people who work for you. Resale or redistribution is not permitted.

Were these built with AI?

Yes, in part. Each template was built with the assistance of AI tools and then reviewed for formula accuracy. The sample data (company names, clients, jobs, people and amounts) is fictional.

No. You pay once on Gumroad, download the file, and it is yours. There is no login, no monthly fee and nothing to cancel.

Can I change the formulas or add rows?

Yes. Nothing is locked or hidden. Insert rows inside the existing tables and the totals pick them up. Each Read Me sheet explains how to add jobs, crew members or invoices without breaking anything.
